Understanding the Two Design Directions

Each concept tests a specific customer psychology and buying path, helping Michael identify the exact feel for his brand before writing a single line of backend production code.

Direction 01

Modern Digital Showroom

A clean, editorial automotive retail aesthetic with crisp white backgrounds, subtle borders, scannable truck specification pills, and consumer-friendly browsing.

  • ✓ Aesthetic: Crisp #FFFFFF canvas, #F4F6F8 panels, automotive red-orange (#D94B32) accents.
  • ✓ Buyer Persona: General truck buyers, family adventurers, everyday drivers seeking transparency.
  • ✓ Core Focus: Clear scannable pricing, easy model comparisons, zero friction navigation.
Direction 02

Commercial & Heavy-Duty Depot

A muscular, high-utility presentation emphasizing heavy-duty capabilities, towing classes, payload metrics, and work-ready fleet reliability.

  • ✓ Aesthetic: Deep slate & charcoal dark headers, high-contrast capability badges, technical typography.
  • ✓ Buyer Persona: Contractors, trade professionals, fleet managers, and heavy-towing enthusiasts.
  • ✓ Unique Feature: Interactive Towing Requirement Matcher to immediately filter by payload/weight needs.
OmniPeak Digital Strategic Takeaway for Michael

Predictable Development Costs & Phased Scalability

Because Michael specifically noted needing to understand development costs before committing, both of these concepts are engineered to start lean:

Phase 1: Direct Inquiries Low setup cost. Vehicles capture warm lead inquiries with zero payment processor fees.
Phase 2: Deposit Checkout Allow customers to place a refundable $500 vehicle reservation deposit securely online.
Phase 3: Live Inventory Sync Integrate DMS or live inventory feeds as Michael's physical lot or supply relationships expand.
