America’s Baseball Classic — Homepage Transformation

Comparing the client's current baseline website with the two complimentary executive mockup concepts.

Concept 1: Grandstand Editorial

Theme: Prestige Championship Heritage & High-Stakes Sports Property.

Cinematic midnight navy, varsity gold accents, classic serif typography (Playfair & Cinzel), asymmetrical layouts, and editorial storytelling. Ideal for high-level sponsor alignment and civic leadership presentations.

Concept 2: Modern Primetime

Theme: Modern High-Energy Sports Media & Broadcast Network.

Carbon obsidian surface, electric patriotic crimson, bold athletic sans-serif (Oswald & Cabinet Grotesk), live telemetry tickers, interactive state switcher, and high-impact broadcast telemetry.

Primary Client Objective Met:

Transitions the perception from a basic information-heavy website to an investor-ready national sports property ready for funding, media contracts, and civic partnerships.
