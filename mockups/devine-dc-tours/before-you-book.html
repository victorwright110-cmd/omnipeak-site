Before You Book
Essential Guest Information

Everything you need to know about our strict 14-seat vehicle limit, check-in arrival times, digital QR phone tickets, weather policies, and same-day rescheduling.

14

Strict 14-Passenger Capacity

Our touring shuttles are strictly limited to 14 guests per departure. We never overbook. This guarantees every passenger an intimate view, ample personal space, and prompt boarding at each monument stop.

⏰

Arrive 10–15 Minutes Prior

Please arrive at the designated meeting point 10 to 15 minutes before your scheduled departure time for digital QR ticket verification. Tours depart promptly to adhere to National Park Service staging schedules.

↺

Same-Day Rescheduling Policy

Travel plans change. You may reschedule your reservation online up to 2 hours before your departure directly through our Reschedule Portal, subject to seat availability on the new slot.

📱

Paperless Mobile QR Tickets

No printing required. Upon completing your online reservation, your digital boarding pass with a scannable QR code is generated instantly and can be presented directly on your smartphone screen to your tour guide.

♿

Accessibility & Step-In Notice

Our shuttle features 2 low, wide steps for easy boarding. We comfortably accommodate ambulatory passengers. Non-motorized, foldable manual wheelchairs and walkers are safely stowed in rear cargo during tours.

🌦️

Rain or Shine Operation

Tours operate in all weather conditions. Our vehicle is fully enclosed with panoramic glass windows, high-capacity air conditioning for warm humid days, and powerful heating for cool evening tours.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Guest Questions

Where is the central departure location? +

For individual daytime and moonlight ticket holders, our designated departure area is centrally located on the National Mall near the Smithsonian Metro and Lincoln Memorial area. Your digital ticket specifies the exact GPS pin and walking instructions.

How does hotel pickup work? +

Complimentary door-to-door hotel pickup within Washington, D.C. city limits is included whenever you book all 14 seats or reserve a Private Full-Vehicle Charter. During online checkout, simply enter your D.C. hotel name and street address.

Can I get off the van at the monuments? +

Yes! Our itineraries include dedicated guided walking stops at the U.S. Capitol, the Washington Monument grounds, and the Lincoln Memorial. Your guide escorts you to prime photo viewpoints and answers your questions.

What is the difference between Daytime ($49) and Moonlight ($59)? +

Daytime tours ($49) highlight the neoclassical architecture and Capitol grounds in bright sunlight. Moonlight tours ($59) depart at 7:30 PM to showcase the Lincoln Memorial, Jefferson Memorial, and Capitol dome dramatically illuminated against the night sky without daytime traffic.
