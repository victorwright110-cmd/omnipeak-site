★ GUEST INFORMATION & POLICIES ★

Before You Book Your Tour

Everything you need to know about our vehicles, boarding guidelines, rescheduling, and hotel pickups.

Accessibility Notice

"Our current tour vehicles are not wheelchair accessible."

Guests must be able to climb two low-entry steps to board the vehicle. Folding walkers and collapsible strollers may be stowed.

Arrival Recommendation

"Please arrive 10–15 minutes before your scheduled tour departure."

Tours depart promptly at the scheduled time to ensure all monuments and photo stops can be enjoyed without rushing.

Missed Departure & Rescheduling

"If you miss your scheduled departure, you may use your ticket for another tour on the same day, subject to seat availability. You can reschedule your booking online."

Mobile Digital QR Tickets

"Instant digital ticket with a secure scannable QR code issued straight to your smartphone for contactless boarding."

Our guide scans your phone directly before entering the shuttle. No printing required.

Private Full-Vehicle Booking & Hotel Pickup

"Hotel pickup is available for customers booking the entire vehicle, provided the hotel is located within the District of Columbia."

Simply select 14 passengers or check "Reserve Entire 14-Seat Vehicle" during checkout, and enter your D.C. hotel address for door-to-door shuttle service.
