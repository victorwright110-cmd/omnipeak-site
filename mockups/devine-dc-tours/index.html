DEVINE DC TOURS (divineddctours.com) • Presentation Brief

Concept 01: Presidential Heritage & Architectural Luxury

Typography: Cormorant Garamond + Plus Jakarta Sans | Palette: Presidential Navy (#102F56), Heritage Gold (#C9A34E), Elegant Ivory (#FAF8F3)

Inspired by luxury boutique travel agencies and high-end Washington DC architectural journals. Features daytime monument grandeur, warm ivory schedule reservation cards, and classic gold monument needle motifs.

Concept 02: Cinematic Twilight & Luminary

Typography: Playfair Display + Manrope | Palette: Rich Midnight Blue (#091B30), Obsidian Navy (#030A12), Glowing Gold

Tailored to highlight the unique enchantment of Washington DC after dark. Brings high-contrast night monument photography to the forefront, giving prominent luminary treatment to the 8:00 PM – 10:00 PM Moonlight Tour.
