Concept 01 Editorial Atelier

The Culinary Atelier

Designed for restaurant owners who pride themselves on cuisine, craft, and dining room atmosphere. It reads like a luxury culinary publication combined with a strategic restaurant growth advisory.

Typography: Newsreader (Editorial Serif) + Plus Jakarta Sans
Color Mood: Deep Charcoal Black (#0C0D0E), Stone Warm Beige (#EFE9E0), Pastel Apricot Orange (#F28C38)
Hero Approach: Asymmetrical split layout with candlelit dining room photography and live "Friday Service 94% Seated" pill.
Best For: Upscale bistros, independent chef-driven venues, fine dining groups, wine bars, boutique hospitality.
Concept 02 Modern Studio

The Modern Culinary Engine

Designed for decisive operators, multi-unit owners, and modern hospitality entrepreneurs who want clear commercial performance, speed, and mathematical proof of table yield.

Typography: Outfit (Geometric Grotesque) + Plus Jakarta Sans
Color Mood: Obsidian Carbon (#08090A), Sand Tan (#ECE4D8), High-Impact Pastel Orange (#FF8A3D)
Hero Approach: Live 6-table floor dynamics matrix + Interactive Table Revenue & Slow-Night Yield Calculator.
Best For: High-volume restaurants, casual dining groups, modern brasseries, operators focused on mid-week numbers.
