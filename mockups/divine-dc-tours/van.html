Vehicle Features & Guest Amenities

🪟

Panoramic Open-Glass Windows

Oversized viewing glass provides unobstructed views of the soaring Washington Monument and U.S. Capitol dome from every seat in the van.

❄️

Dual Climate Heating & A/C

Powerful overhead air conditioning cools you quickly during hot summer tours, while efficient heating keeps evening moonlight tours crisp and cozy.

Low-Step Easy Boarding

Designed with senior and family accessibility in mind. Only two low, wide steps ensure effortless boarding and disembarking at every monument stop.

Our current vehicles are not wheelchair accessible. Shuttle accommodates ambulatory passengers via 2 low step-in levels. Collapsible walker stowage available.

Studio PA Audio System

Distributed ceiling audio ensures every story, presidential anecdote, and historical insight from your guide is heard with crystal-clear clarity.

Free Hotel Pickup on Full Bus

When you reserve the entire 14-passenger vehicle, complimentary door-to-door pickup from any Washington, D.C. hotel is included at no extra charge.
