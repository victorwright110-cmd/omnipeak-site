OmniPeak Digital Creative Brief & Execution

Divine DC Tours (Client: Shantal Downer) • Response to Design Feedback

Direct Response to Client Feedback:

The previous mockup concepts were noted as too dark, dull, and lacking visual excitement. In this revised release, we eliminated dark overlays and heavy backgrounds in favor of generous warm white (#FFFFFF), bright daylight sunlit photography of the Washington Monument and U.S. Capitol, vibrant royal blue (#123C78), and polished gold accents.

Concept 01: Sunlit Capital Grandeur & Vibrant Tourism

Palette: Royal Blue (#123C78), Heritage Gold (#C9A24A), Warm White (#FFFFFF), Soft Ivory (#FAF8F3)

A welcoming American sightseeing experience. Features full-width sun-drenched monument photography, spotlight on the 14-passenger boutique touring vehicle experience with happy tourists, and an intuitive online reservation engine.

Concept 02: Modern Editorial Concierge & Dynamic Blue

Palette: Crisp White, Sky Tint (#EAF3FB), Deep Royal Blue, Heritage Gold

A sleek, modern editorial travel aesthetic with multi-card landmark photography, an interactive comparison table highlighting the advantages of a 14-passenger boutique vehicle over generic 55-person buses, and live departure slot booking.

Strict 14-Passenger Capacity Logic Implemented:

Every departure slot (10:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 4:00 PM, 7:30 PM) is bounded by an independent 14-passenger inventory. When 14 confirmed seats are logged in persistence, that slot immediately locks to prevent overselling. Test bookings can be inspected or reset anytime via the fleet roster tool.
