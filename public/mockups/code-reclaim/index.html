Independent Opportunities

Where We See Immediate Growth Opportunities

Rather than generic marketing tactics, our strategy focuses on five concrete, high-leverage commercial areas that directly impact Jackie’s stated goals: brand visibility and activewear sales.

01

Increase Commercial Purpose & Publishing Consistency

Social posting without clear commercial intent leaves engagement disconnected from store traffic. Every scheduled feed post and reel should serve a specific purpose in the buyer journey: introducing a signature cut (e.g. Unstoppable Halter Top), showcasing fabric movement, answering fit questions, or guiding followers directly to a curated collection page with a compelling call to action.

02

Create Product-Focused Short-Form Creative & Lifestyle Storytelling

Short-form vertical video (Instagram Reels) remains the most powerful organic reach engine on Meta platforms. We recommend systematically transforming existing and new model footage into high-retention video formats: 3-way styling clips, sweat-proof workout stress tests, color-pairing guides, and day-to-night transitions that illustrate versatility beyond the studio.

03

Introduce Structured Facebook & Instagram Advertising Tests

Organic social alone rarely generates predictable e-commerce scale. A disciplined Meta advertising program allows Code Reclaim to systematically target activewear enthusiasts, fitness instructors, and pilates/athleisure buyers in specific zip codes and demographic tiers, testing which creative angles drive the most cost-effective add-to-carts and purchases.

04

High-Leverage Opportunity: Coordinated Set Bundling & Free Shipping Synergy

Verified Store Math: Code Reclaim proudly offers Free Shipping on orders over $60. Single tops are priced at $35 and bottoms at $35–$38. When a customer buys only one item, they fall below the free shipping threshold and face shipping friction. By actively merchandising and advertising “Coordinated 2-Piece Sets” (e.g. Adaptive Top + Shorts for $73), customers immediately unlock free shipping, increasing Average Order Value (AOV) by over 90% while providing superior customer satisfaction.

05

Amplify the Existing Ambassador Program & Creator-Led Assets

Code Reclaim already maintains an active ambassador intake application on its website. We see an immediate opportunity to activate this existing asset: structuring outreach to micro-creators, coordinating gifted sets for verified review videos, and repurposing authentic ambassador try-ons into high-converting Meta advertising creative.
