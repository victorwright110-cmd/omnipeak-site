The Strategic Thesis

Baseball has always been more than a game.

“Fans want more than a seat in a stadium. They want an experience, a connection to the event, and something worth talking about long after the final score.”

Kelly Kleinman • Founder, America’s Baseball Classic

A smaller affiliated minor league system and a shorter draft have left thousands of capable, entertaining American players without a national stage.

America’s Baseball Classic unites professional-level talent, deep-rooted state rivalries, live musical entertainment, and regional media to deliver an unforgettable sports experience designed for families, investors, and broad audiences.

Authentic Sport

Uncompromising Standard

The Game Always Comes First

The talent on the field, the strategy in the dugout, the tension of a meaningful 3-2 count, and the pursuit of a championship remain at the absolute center.

We do not need to change the essential nature of baseball. We need to build a more compelling event around it. Managers are focused on winning. Players compete at their highest professional level. Entertainment belongs in pregame celebrations, between-inning spectacles, and postgame events — enhancing the atmosphere rather than distracting from the 9 innings.

Competitive Integrity Preserved

No gimmick rules. True professional baseball played by determined athletes.

Cultural Entertainment Layer
