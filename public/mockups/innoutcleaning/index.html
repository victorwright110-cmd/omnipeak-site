Concept 01 · Fresh Entry Luxury Editorial

“A Cleaner Home. A Better Day.”

Designed for affluent homeowners in Shadyside, Squirrel Hill, and Mt. Lebanon. Uses editorial serif typography (Cormorant Garamond), cinematic photography, soft rounded cards, and an interactive “From Doorstep to Done” doorway journey with a live before/after slider.

Best For: Premium residential private clients & recurring housekeeper contracts.
Concept 02 · IN-N-OUT Signature Modern Startup

“IN. CLEAN. OUT. DONE.”

Designed for high-speed conversion with a split-screen hero, prominent clickable phone 412-525-1998, oversized numeric stats, interactive room cards, a 4-phase “You’re In. We Clean. You’re Out.” lifestyle timeline, and an instant cleaning price estimator.

Best For: Busy working professionals, tech families & rapid booking velocity.
