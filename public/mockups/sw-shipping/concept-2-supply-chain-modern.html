SERVICES MATRIX

Complete Logistics Solutions

Precision-managed multimodal logistics connecting origin to final destination.

01. Freight Forwarding

Integrated freight solutions supporting international cargo movement. Comprehensive carrier bookings, customs clearing, and multimodal transfer coordination.

02. Sea Freight

Ocean freight solutions for businesses moving cargo across international markets. Full Container Load (FCL) and Less Than Container Load (LCL) options.

03. Air Freight

Air cargo solutions designed for time-sensitive shipping requirements. Scheduled flights, fast customs processing, and expedited airport delivery.

04. Consolidation

Efficient consolidation services for shipments from international origins. Group multiple consignments into single container runs to minimize costs.

05. Road Transportation

Dependable inland transportation connecting cargo with its destination. Bonded container trucking from coastal terminals to upcountry hubs across Pakistan.

06. Warehousing & Storage

Flexible storage and warehousing support for logistics operations. Staging yards, inventory distribution, cross-docking, and secure warehousing.
