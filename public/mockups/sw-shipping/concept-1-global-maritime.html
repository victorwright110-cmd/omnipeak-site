WHO WE ARE

Your Trusted Partner in Global Freight & Logistics

Established in May 2012 and headquartered in the commercial core of Karachi, Pakistan, SW SHIPPING (PVT.) LTD. was founded by industry leader Asif Rizvi under a straightforward, enduring philosophy: "Smart People – Great Solutions."

Over more than a decade of proven operations, we have evolved into a comprehensive freight forwarding and supply chain partner. We streamline complex international trade operations through multimodal sea freight, scheduled air cargo, specialized consolidation, nationwide inland transportation, and warehousing.

Whether handling commercial consignments through Port of Karachi and Port Qasim or coordinating time-critical air freight across Asia, the Middle East, and beyond, our experienced logistics specialists deliver precision, regulatory compliance, and total accountability.

STREAMLINED EXECUTION

From Your First Inquiry to Final Delivery

Our four-stage logistics methodology guarantees predictable scheduling, rigorous customs compliance, and cargo security.

STEP 01

Inquiry & Rate Quotation

We review your cargo specifications (FCL, LCL, air express, or bulk), origin-destination parameters, and formulate competitive, transparent freight rates.

✓ Quick quote response within 24 hours
STEP 02

Planning & Documentation

Carrier booking confirmation, Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill (AWB) issuance, export clearance documentation, and customs compliance prep.

✓ Complete customs brokerage handling
STEP 03

Multimodal Transit & Monitoring

Safe vessel or aircraft departure from Port of Karachi, Port Qasim, or airport. Proactive milestone notifications and transit milestone reporting.

✓ Continuous corridor status oversight
STEP 04

Final Clearance & Safe Delivery

Port handling at destination, import customs discharge, bonded road haulage or warehouse staging, and timely handover to the consignee.

✓ Proof of delivery and final reconciliation
